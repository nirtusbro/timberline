How to play

  • Carve. Drag left and right anywhere on the lower screen. Sharp turns cost speed, so keep them smooth.
  • Gates. Pass between each pair of flags. Every gate in a row raises the combo, up to ×8. A missed gate or a tumble resets it.
  • Bullseyes. Thread the glowing spot in the middle of a gate for +50, a coin and a carve boost: a burst of speed. Chain them and you fly.
  • Stars. Each level has three star scores. One star opens the next level; stars open new mountains.
  • Power-ups. Magnet pulls coins in. Shield takes one knock. ×2 doubles every point for eight seconds. Supply crates hold them too.
  • The mountain. Overtake learners, pass behind deer, keep clear of snowcats. Hit a ramp straight to fly over rock. On blue ice the skis barely turn: set your line first.
  • Endless and Daily. Three lives; every miss or tumble costs one, every ten crates win one back. The daily course is the same for everyone, and new each day.

On a keyboard: A / D or ← / → to steer, Esc or P to pause.
