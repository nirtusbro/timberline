How to play

  • CarveDrag left and right. Let go and the skis follow the trail. Sharp turns cost speed.
  • GatesThe flags mark the way down. Pass between them; each in a row builds the combo.
  • BullseyeThread the glowing spot for a burst of speed.
  • StarsTwo forgive one miss. Three want every gate, no tumble, and enough bullseyes.
  • Power-upsMagnet, shield and ×2. Crates hold them too.
  • The lineRocks, skiers and animals stand on it. Read them early and pass behind whatever is crossing.

Keyboard: A / D or ← / → steer, Esc pauses, R restarts.
